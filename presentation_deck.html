The Operational Reality

The "2-Day" Manual Payroll Bottleneck

Current friction points faced every semi-monthly cutoff across your 3 busy salon branches.

⏳ 2 Days Lost in Manual Math

Takes 16+ hours every cutoff for management to gather raw attendance logs, calculate daily hours, and tally attendance for every employee across 3 locations.

⚠️ Calculation Risks & Errors

Manual computation of intricate rules:

  • 5-minute grace periods
  • 10-minute deduction increments
  • Overtime & Night Differential
  • SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG statutory formulas

Errors cause employee disputes and tedious payroll adjustments.

📋 Scattered Biometrics & Compliance

Raw NGTeco logs live on loose USB sticks. Missing clock-outs require investigative back-and-forth, while habitual tardiness lacks automated Notice to Explain (NTE) compliance.
