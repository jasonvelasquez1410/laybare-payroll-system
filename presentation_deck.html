The Operational Reality

The "2-Day" Manual Payroll Bottleneck

Current challenges faced every cutoff across your 3 busy salon branches.

⏳ 2 Days Spent on Manual Math

Currently takes 16+ hours every cutoff for management to gather raw attendance logs, calculate daily hours, and tally attendance for every employee across 3 locations.

⚠️ Discrepancies & Calculation Risks

Manual computation of intricate rules:

  • 5-minute grace periods
  • 10-minute deduction increments
  • Overtime & Night Differential
  • SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG formulas

Errors lead to employee disputes and time-consuming adjustments.

📋 Scattered Biometrics & Compliance

Raw NGTeco logs live on USB drives and individual computers. Missing clock-outs require back-and-forth investigation, while habitual tardiness lacks automated Notice to Explain (NTE) tracking.

The Solution

From 2 Days to 5 Minutes

A purpose-built web platform engineered specifically for ALRAJJ LEGACY salon operations.

📂

1. Direct NGTeco Ingestion

Simply drag and drop the biometric Excel file exported from your branches. Automatic employee mapping in seconds.

⚡

2. Smart Anomaly Engine

Instantly highlights missing clock-outs or unpaired punches. HR can review and approve overrides with full audit logs.

⚖️

3. Automated NTE & Rules

Enforces your exact 5-min grace and 10-min deduction rules. Flags 3+ late staff and generates ready-to-print NTE letters.

💰

4. 1-Click Payroll & Payslips

Calculates Gross Pay, Statutory Deductions, and Net Pay in 1 second. Generates printable itemized employee payslips.
