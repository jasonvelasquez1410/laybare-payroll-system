1. Project Overview

This proposal outlines the development and implementation of a Custom Smart Payroll System engineered specifically to eliminate manual computation time for ALRAJJ LEGACY Fortified Business Corp. The system will initially be deployed to centralize attendance and payroll for three (3) active locations: Centrio (covering both Waxing Salon and Passion Nails), Ketkai, and SM Downtown.

Note: The Iligan branch is excluded from this initial deployment, but the system's architecture will allow for seamless integration once operational.

2. Scope of Work & Key Features

To transition from a 2-day manual process to an automated workflow, the system will include:

  • Biometric Data Integration: A smart importer module designed to read and process raw attendance logs exported directly from your existing NGTeco biometric devices.
  • Automated Rules Engine: Automatic computation of regular hours, overtime, night differential, and statutory deductions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG).
  • Custom Tardiness Tracking: Hardcoded logic to apply your specific 5-minute grace period and 10-minute deduction rule, alongside a habitual tardiness tracker capable of generating Notices to Explain (NTEs).
  • HR Exception Dashboard: A centralized portal for the HR Officer to review flagged anomalies (e.g., missing time-outs) before finalizing the batch payroll.
