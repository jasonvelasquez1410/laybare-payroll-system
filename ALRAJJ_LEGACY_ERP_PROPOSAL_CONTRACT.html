1 Executive Summary & Value Proposition

ALRAJJ LEGACY Fortified Business Corp. is upgrading its multi-branch operations from vulnerable, manual spreadsheets into an all-in-one, cloud-synchronized Enterprise Biometric HRMS, POS CRM, Procurement, Accounting, and Cloud DMS & E-Signature Ecosystem.

This enterprise software eliminates human payroll errors, automates DOLE compliance notices and BPI BizLink batch disbursements, prevents salon cash shrinkage, connects franchise commissary purchase orders (MyTime) directly with Accounting AP via 3-Way Match, and provides a centralized paperless legal archive with Google Workspace integration.
